THE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

How the Marketplace Operates

Seamless bridge connecting clients with verified local salon owners.

01

Client Booking Request

Clients browse unisex services, select their preferred slot, and place an order on the platform.

02

Nearby Salon Routing

The platform algorithm instantly routes the order to the closest registered partner salon within their service radius (e.g. 5km).

03

At-Home Execution

The salon owner dispatches a verified top stylist with sanitized kits to deliver the grooming session at the client's home.
